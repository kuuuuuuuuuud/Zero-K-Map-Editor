    Turn on advanced mode?
    
    
    Advanced mode removes the safe limits of every map setting. You can then build maps that are
      unplayable, unbalanced or broken: bases nobody can reach, metal spots inside walls, huge build times,
      maps that fail to build, lag or crash Zero-K.
    
    
    The check report still warns you, but nothing stops you. Use it at your own risk and test such maps
      before you share them.
